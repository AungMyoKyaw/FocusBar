Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026 · FocusBar for macOS

Overview

FocusBar is a local-first macOS menu bar Pomodoro timer. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit your personal data, focus history, or usage analytics to any external server.

Data Stored on Your Mac

  • Focus Sessions & Statistics: Your completed sessions, streaks, XP, and achievements are stored locally on your Mac using Apple's SwiftData framework and UserDefaults.
  • Preferences: Timer durations, soundscape settings, and UI options remain strictly on your device.
  • Exports: If you export your data to JSON or CSV, the file is saved only to the location you explicitly choose.

Optional System Permissions

  • Apple Reminders (EventKit): Only accessed if you choose to connect Apple Reminders to link focus sessions to tasks or mark reminders complete. Reminder data never leaves your Mac.
  • Apple Calendar (EventKit): Only accessed if you enable Calendar Sync to log completed focus blocks or view upcoming events. Calendar data never leaves your Mac.
  • Notifications: Used solely to alert you locally when a focus or break interval finishes.

Third-Party Services & Tracking

FocusBar contains no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising frameworks, and no user tracking.

Support & Contact

For bug reports, feature requests, or privacy questions, please open an issue on GitHub Issues or visit the FocusBar homepage.
